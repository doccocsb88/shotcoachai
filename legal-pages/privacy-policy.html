  • Purchase and entitlement state: product identifiers, subscription status, transaction status, restore state, and premium access state provided by Apple App Store, Google Play, or another authorized payment platform.
  • Device and app information: device type, operating system version, app version, language, region, network state, and diagnostic information needed for app functionality, compatibility, security, or support.
  • Network request data: technical information normally transmitted over the internet, such as IP address, request time, device/network metadata, and hosting logs handled by platform or service providers.

Photos, Camera, Files, and User Content

Some apps may let you capture photos, choose images from your library, import files, or create user content. Some apps may offer AI-powered features. When you choose to use those features, the app may send the photo, image, prompt, instruction, or related input you provide to third-party AI service providers to generate, edit, analyze, classify, translate, or otherwise process the requested result.
